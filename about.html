🌍 About CHO22

Passionate about plants, driven by design

Our Story

CHO22 Plant Design was founded with a simple belief: plants make life better. We started as a small plant shop and have grown into a full-service plant design company serving homes and businesses across Sydney.

Our Mission

To bring the beauty and benefits of plants into every space. We design with intention, care for each plant, and create environments that inspire and refresh.

Our Values

  • 🌱 Sustainability — We prioritize eco-friendly materials and practices.
  • 🎨 Creativity — Every project is a unique expression of nature and design.
  • ❤️ Care — We treat every plant and client with genuine attention.
